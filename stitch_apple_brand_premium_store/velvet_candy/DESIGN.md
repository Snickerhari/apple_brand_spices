# Velvet Candy — Sophisticated & Expressive

## North Star: "Joyful Sophistication"
Bold, expressive, and refined. Muted jewel tones, pill-shaped elements, and smooth, fluid transitions. Designed to feel premium yet playful.

## Colors
- **Primary (`#ad5b93`):** Muted Mauve — primary actions and brand identity.
- **Secondary (`#537f77`):** Sage Teal — secondary elements, navigation, and categories.
- **Tertiary (`#0ca98f`):** Aqua Teal — informational callouts, links, and vibrant highlights.
- **Background (`#fdf9fa`):** Warm White — tinted with the neutral rose for a cohesive, soft feel.
- Use the accent colors to create a sophisticated, expressive rhythm. The palette is rich but balanced.

## Typography
- **All fonts:** DM Sans — rounded, friendly, modern.
- Use bold weight for headings, medium for labels. Generous line-height for readability.
- Slightly larger base size (16px body) to maintain a friendly, accessible presence.

## Shapes & Motion
- **Border radius:** Full/pill on buttons and badges. 24-32px on cards and large containers.
- **Microinteractions:** Smooth, fluid transitions (`transform: scale(1.02)`, ease-out timing).
- **Shadows:** Tinted — use soft, diffused shadows matching the primary or neutral colors at 15-20% opacity.
  Example: a mauve button gets `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(173, 91, 147, 0.2)`.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Pill-shaped, solid fill, tinted mauve shadow. Hover = subtle lift + increased shadow spread.
- **Cards:** Large radius (32px), white fill, warm tinted shadow. Hover = gentle scale animation.
- **Badges/Tags:** Pill-shaped, muted sage or aqua fill, bold text.
- **Inputs:** Fully rounded (pill radius), light fill, sage-teal focus ring.

## Rules
- Focus on tonal harmony. While expressive, the colors should never feel jarring or "neon."
- Rounded shapes are mandatory — avoid sharp angles to keep the system feeling soft and approachable.
- Animations should be fluid and graceful, avoiding the aggressive "bounciness" of more juvenile systems.
